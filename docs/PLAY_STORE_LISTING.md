## Full description

Give your brain a satisfying block puzzle break with GridBlast.

Fit colorful shapes into an 8×8 board. Complete rows or columns to clear them and earn points. Use all three shapes to draw a fresh tray, plan each move, and try to beat your personal best.

Play your way:
- Drag shapes directly onto the board, or tap a piece and choose a space.
- Ask for a free Hint and confirm the suggested move with PLACE.
- Undo your previous move, even after a game over.
- Keep your score and puzzle progress on your device.
- Set sound, vibration, and animations to your preference.

GridBlast is made for relaxing, thoughtful offline puzzle play. No ads, subscriptions, accounts, internet connection or tracking.

GridBlast is an independent original game, not affiliated with another block puzzle brand.
