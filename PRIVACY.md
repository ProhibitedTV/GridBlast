# GridBlast Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

GridBlast is an independent, ad-free, offline Android puzzle game developed by ProhibitedTV.

## What the game collects

GridBlast does **not** collect, transmit, share, or sell personal information. It has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, user account, cloud sync, telemetry, or network permission.

## On-device storage

GridBlast stores your current puzzle board, one-step undo checkpoint, score, high score, and sound/vibration/animation preferences **only in private storage on your Android device**. The application disables Android cloud backup and declares exclusion rules for Android device-to-device transfer. This information is not transmitted to us or to any server. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game deletes these local records.

## Permissions

GridBlast does not request internet access, advertising ID access, or location access. Network cleartext traffic is disabled in the app manifest. Device haptics and local audio are controlled by Android and your in-game preferences.

## Children

GridBlast is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app does not transmit personal information.

## Updates to this policy

If privacy-relevant functionality changes, this policy will be updated before that functionality is distributed.

## Contact

For questions or concerns, open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/ProhibitedTV/GridBlast/issues.

This policy describes GridBlast's application behavior. GitHub, app stores, and operating systems may process their own separate diagnostic or service data under their own policies.
